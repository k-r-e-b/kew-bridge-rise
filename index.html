The apartment

Olive velvet, warm brass, soft light, and views over the rooftops of West London.

This one-bedroom apartment has been furnished with care. The open-plan living area has a deep velvet corner sofa that converts into a sofa bed, a marble-effect dining table with wishbone chairs for four, and full-height glass doors that open onto a private balcony.

The kitchen comes with an integrated oven, microwave and induction hob, plus a coffee machine and everything you need to cook properly. The double bedroom has a boucle arched headboard, full-width mirrored wardrobes and its own door to the balcony.

The building is newly built and has a landscaped residents' courtyard garden. It is a few minutes' walk from Kew Bridge station, Kew Gardens and the Thames.
